\begin{frame}{\textbf{5}\; Constructions come in pairs}
\begin{columns}[c]
\begin{column}{0.56\textwidth}
One primitive: a three-way join whose \alert{mixed} part is the parameter.
\renewcommand{\AgdaCodeStyle}{\footnotesize}
\begin{code}
data _⋄_⋄_ (Γ₁ : WRel A) (Γ₂ : WRel B) (Γ₃ : WRel (A ⊎ B))
           : WRel (A ⊎ B) where
  left  : Γ₁ u v → (Γ₁ ⋄ Γ₂ ⋄ Γ₃) [ u ]ₗ [ v ]ₗ
  right : Γ₂ u v → (Γ₁ ⋄ Γ₂ ⋄ Γ₃) [ u ]ᵣ [ v ]ᵣ
  mid   : Γ₃ u v → (Γ₁ ⋄ Γ₂ ⋄ Γ₃) u v
\end{code}
\vspace{2pt}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
mixed part & construction\\
\midrule
\aid{CommRel} & direct product\\
\aid{ConjRelʷ} \textit{conj} & semidirect product\\
\aid{AmalgRel} $f_1\,f_2$ & amalgamated free product\\
\aid{SugarRel} & definitional extension\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.41\textwidth}
\small
\textbf{Syntactic}: normal forms combine --- for $\times$ and $\rtimes$ the carrier is
$\mathit{NF}_1\times\mathit{NF}_2$; an amalgam has a base normal form and
alternating cosets:
\renewcommand{\AgdaCodeStyle}{\scriptsize}
\begin{code}[hide]
amalNFC : Set → Set → Set
\end{code}
\begin{code}
amalNFC C D = (D ⊎ ⊤) × List (C × D) × (C ⊎ ⊤)
\end{code}
\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Semantic}: if the factors present $G_1$, $G_2$, the construction presents
$G_1\times G_2$, $G_1\rtimes G_2$, $G_1\ast_{G_0}G_2$ --- and group extensions.
\vspace{4pt}

{\scriptsize Semidirect products, amalgamated free products of monoids and group
extensions were not in agda-stdlib; we contribute them (\texttt{ForStdlib}, 865 lines).}
