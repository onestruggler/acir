------------------------------------------------------------------------
-- Presentations of groups
--
-- Lemma A.1: (d3) at the indices 0 … 3 (Clément, Appendix A.2)
--
--     (H_[2,3] H_[0,2] H_[1,3])⁴  ≈  H_[0,1] H_[2,3]
--
-- The paper's chain of eighteen lines, from (d3*): signs on 2, 3 are
-- inserted and turned into the exchanges X_[0,2] X_[1,3] (b4, d2, b5),
-- which carry H_[2,3] to H_[0,1] (c5†, c4†) and are turned back into
-- signs (b5, d2†, b4), twice per half; then (d3*) applies and what is
-- left cancels.  Each line is one or a few structured moves — commuting
-- swaps, an exchange passing a letter it renames (LemmaA1.Rename), (d1),
-- (d2), (d2†) — generated from the paper's lines and checked
-- numerically, every intermediate line against the paper's (LemmaA1/gen:
-- gen_chain.py, macros.py, spec_d3.py).
------------------------------------------------------------------------
