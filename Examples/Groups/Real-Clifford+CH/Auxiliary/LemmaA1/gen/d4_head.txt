------------------------------------------------------------------------
-- Presentations of groups
--
-- Lemma A.1: (d4) at the indices 0 … 5 (Clément, Appendix A.2)
--
--     (H_[0,4] H_[1,5] H_[0,1] H_[0,4] H_[1,5] X_[4,2] X_[5,3])³
--       ≈  H_[4,2] H_[5,3] H_[2,3] H_[4,2] H_[5,3] X_[4,2] X_[5,3]
--
-- The paper's chain of twenty lines, from (d4*): the exchanges
-- X_[4,2] X_[5,3] of the first period carry the second across (b5, c5)
-- and cancel those of the second; signs on 0, 1, 4, 5 are inserted and
-- turned into X_[0,4] X_[1,5] and back, around (d4*), and the same is
-- done with signs on 2, 3 at the end.  Each line is one or a few
-- structured moves, generated from the paper's lines and checked
-- numerically, every intermediate line against the paper's (LemmaA1/gen:
-- gen_chain.py, macros.py, spec_d4.py).  Cut into segments of a few
-- steps, as LemmaA1.D3.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
