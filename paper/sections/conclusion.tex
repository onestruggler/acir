\section{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented an Agda library for presentations of monoids and groups,
normal forms, and completeness of finite relation sets, organized around
inductively defined circuits and a verified, monoid-level
Reidemeister--Schreier engine.  Completeness is factored, once and for
all, into soundness plus a unique-normal-form witness; normal forms are
built by verified coset tables, one digit per level of a subgroup tower,
or glued by amalgamation when the tower branches.

The work continues in two directions.  \emph{More completeness
results.}  The gate-set rule sets surveyed in \S\ref{sec:rel-paper} are
finite relation sets with known normal forms, exactly the input the
library is built to verify, and each still awaits a machine-checked
proof.  \emph{Upstreaming.}  The \texttt{ForStdlib} constructions are being
prepared for the Agda standard library, whose conventions the whole
library already follows.

Completeness proofs for circuit calculi are growing --- three hundred
pages of appendix is no longer
exceptional~\cite{clement2026realcliffordch}.  This library is evidence
that the right infrastructure --- presentations as data, normal forms
as bundles, coset tables as certificates --- turns such proofs from
heroic manuscripts into ordinary, reusable, machine-checked mathematics.
