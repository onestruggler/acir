\section{The Framework, in Brief}\label{sec:framework}

The library~\cite{bian2026framework,qupit-code} presents monoids and
groups by generators and relations; syntax is unquotiented, so normal
forms and coset tables are programs, and every construction comes with
a presentation theorem against a concrete group.  \aid{{\_}≈{\_}} is the
monoid congruence generated on words by the axioms \aid{{\_}==={\_}},
the word problem of $\pres{X}{\Gamma}$; a \aid{Grouplike} witness
upgrades to the presented group, and $\Gamma\;\aid{IsPresentationOf}\;G$
packages it with $\sem{\_} : \aid{Word}\;X \to G$ and a group
isomorphism from the presented group onto $G$.
A normal form comes with a \emph{section} $\aid{inv-nf} : \NF \to
\aid{Word}\;X$ realising each normal form as a canonical word;
\aid{UniqueNormalForm} says representatives with equal
denotations are equal, and the five-line \aid{by-normalization} turns
uniqueness and soundness into completeness, so each case study proves
\aid{unique}, about finite data, never about arbitrary words.

\emph{Coset tables.}  Normal forms follow the chain-of-subgroups
recipe of computational group
theory~\cite{sims1994computation,holt2005handbook}, the chain for
circuits being the wire structure.  One level is a Reidemeister--Schreier situation
--- a subgroup presentation $\Gamma$, a group presentation $\Delta$, a
finite type $C$ of coset labels with identity $I$, a section $[\_] : C
\to \aid{Word}\;Y$, the embedding $f$, and the \emph{coset table} $h : C
\to Y \to \aid{Word}\;X \times C$, pushing one letter past a
representative and returning the subgroup residual and the new coset
--- and the library \emph{verifies} a claimed table against five
hypotheses
(Appendix~\ref{app:agda}): the table inverts the embedding at $I$, it
respects $\Delta$'s axioms coset by coset, $f$ respects $\Gamma$'s,
$[I] \approx \varepsilon$, and the \emph{soundness law} $[c] \bullet
[b]\aid{ʷ} \approx (f\,\aid{ʷ})\,b' \bullet [c']$ for $(b', c') =
h\;c\;b$; from these it derives $\aid{NormalForm}\;\Gamma\;\NF \to
\aid{NormalForm}\;\Delta\;(\NF \times C)$, and \aid{CosetTower}
iterates up an $\mathbb{N}$-indexed family, one level per wire.  For semidirect products the mixed relation
$\aid{ConjRelʷ}\;\mathit{conj}$ says $h \bullet n = \mathit{conj}\;h\;n
\bullet h$ with a \emph{word}-valued action, and the semidirect theorem
takes the factors' relation sets, the action, two well-definedness
conditions (it respects the acting group's axioms in its first
argument and the normal subgroup's in its second) and presentations of
the factors, and returns a presentation of the semidirect product of
the \emph{semantic} groups; \aid{extension-presentation} (Handbook~\cite{holt2005handbook},
Proposition~2.55) also corrects each relator of the
quotient by a word of the kernel, and is proved for central extensions
built from a cocycle.  The library's warm-ups --- $S_n$ by a tower of swap staircases, all
hypotheses but the soundness law closing by evaluation, and
$\mathbb{Z}_N \wr S_n$ by the semidirect theorem with finite case
splits --- are the two patterns the Clifford group combines.
