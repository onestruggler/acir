\section{Discussion, Related Work and Conclusion}\label{sec:discussion}

Everything above is checked by Agda~2.8 with standard library 2.4
under \texttt{--safe}: no postulates, no holes.  The development is
113\,526 lines in 349 files (Table~\ref{tab:stats}): framework
15\,000, qupit-specific 60\,000 (symplectic 35\,000, composition
23\,000).

Not formalised are the two well-known \emph{glue facts}: that the
projective Clifford group is $\PPauli \rtimes \Sp$, and that the
Clifford group is a central extension of it by
$\langle\omega\rangle$ (times $\langle -1\rangle$ over
$-\omega$)~\cite{bian2026qudit}; no matrix appears in the
development.

We have used versions of Claude to improve the language of
this paper and to help with the formalisation: composing box relations
to prove \aid{ract-sound}, defining the symplectic group, and
establishing the equivalence between the code's relations and the
qupit paper's Figure~1 modulo scalars; \aid{presentation-exact} and
the Figure~1 layer with $-1$ were done by Claude.

\emph{Related work.}  The completeness proofs for these gate groups,
from Selinger's $n$-qubit Clifford presentation to the qupit rules,
are pen-and-paper (\S\ref{sec:intro}); mechanised completeness is
rare: $\Pi$ as a free symmetric rig
groupoid~\cite{choudhury2022symmetries} and its square-root
extension~\cite{carette2024squareroots} are coherence arguments.
Verified compilers and
logics~\cite{paykin2017qwire,hietala2021voqc,lehmann2025vyzx,zhou2023coqq,chareton2021qbricks,li2022vqo,peduri2025qbc}
prove soundness of rewrites, never completeness, and
mathlib~\cite{mathlib2020,mathlib-doc-presented}, the
AFP~\cite{breitner2010freegroups,kharim2023freegroups} and
MathComp~\cite{gonthier2013oddorder} have no verified coset enumeration
against a given concrete group.

\emph{Next}: a verified matrix semantics over
$U_{p^n}(\mathbb{Z}[1/p,\omega])$, which would close the chain and
discharge the glue facts; the qubit
case~\cite{selinger2015clifford}, which the odd-prime development
does not cover; the qutrit rules of~\cite{li2025qutrit}, to be
reached from ours at $p = 3$; and submission to the Agda standard
library.
