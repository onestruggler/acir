# Real-Clifford+CH (Clément 2026)

A. Clément, *A Complete Equational Theory for Real-Clifford+CH Quantum
Circuits*, arXiv:2602.06644.  Table 1 row: real Clifford+CH,
sub-presentation.  Gates `H`, `Z`, `CZ`, `CH`; the equations of Figure 4
present, at every width `n`, the group of orthogonal matrices (inside
`O_{2^n}(ℤ[1/√2])`) generated by `n`-wire circuits (Theorem 8.9).

Formalized from the paper only.

`QCert.RealCliffordCH.presentation (n : ℕ) :
  IsSubPresentationOf (theory n) (S.gen (n := n))` in `Theorem.lean`.

## Conventions and correspondence with the paper

* Wire `0` is the bottom wire of a drawing (the least significant bit, the
  last tensor factor); on a `k`-wire drawing the top wire is wire `k - 1`.
  `CH` has its control on wire `1`, its `H` on wire `0`, as drawn in (A).
  The basis state `|x, y⟩` of Definition 2.3 (`x` on the upper wire) has
  index `2x + y`.
* Words are in operator order, but every circuit is written with `drawn`,
  whose list is in the order of the drawing (left to right), so each
  relation reads as its figure.
* PROP vs PRO.  The paper's circuits form a PROP (the swap is a generator
  and Figure 1's topological axioms hold).  The framework is a PRO
  (`Lift`: shifts, disjoint gates commute).  Following Remark 1 we add
  Equations (f1)–(f4) (Figure 2, replacing (f)) and (g) of Figure 1, and we
  eliminate the swap generator by Tietze with Equation (8) as its
  definition (`swap`).  So the rules are Figure 4's (1)–(7), (9)–(19), with
  `swap` the circuit of (8), plus (f1)–(f4) and (g).  Remark 1 says "there
  are only four equations to add"; its first sentence adds (f) and (g), and
  we keep (g) (it holds semantically; whether it is derivable from the
  others was not checked).
* Gates on non-adjacent wires (`CZ`/`CH` between wires 2 and 0, used in
  (14)–(17)) and multi-controlled gates with their target not at the bottom
  are defined in the paper by conjugation with swaps, the network being
  irrelevant up to the topological rules.  We fix one network for each:
  conjugation by the swap of wires `0, 1` (`cz20`, `ch20`, and in `zx`), and
  by the transposition of wires `0, 2` (`tau02 = swap · swap↑ · swap`) in the
  recursive step of `box`, keeping the vertical order of the controls.
* The schema (19) is `Rule.e19 k` on `n = k + 5` wires; `box k` has its box
  on wire `0` and its controls on wires `1, …, k`.
* All equations were checked numerically against the transcription before
  being proved (all hold).

## What is proved

* Soundness of every equation (Proposition 3.1), including the schema (19)
  at every width, with no `sorry`.
* Every generator is invertible in the theory.
* Completeness on 0 and 1 wires (Lemma 7.1).
* On 2 wires (Lemma 7.6): the encoding preserves the semantics, Lemma 7.4,
  Lemma 7.5 (every equation of Figure 6, with the auxiliary equations
  (81)–(110)), and Lemma 7.6 from them and Theorem 4.4.  Theorem 4.4,
  which the paper imports from Hadamard-Π [17], is imported from the
  Hadamard-Π folder (`theorem_4_4 := (QCert.HadamardPi.theorem_4_14 4).complete`);
  that source theorem is proved without `sorry`.
* On `n ≥ 3` wires: the encoding of Definition 8.2 produces proper words and
  preserves the semantics in the basis ordered by the Gray code (`esem`).
* Theorem 4.10 (`Sec8/Thm410Proof.lean`, `theorem_4_10`, axioms `propext`,
  `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only), from the two hypotheses of the
  Reidemeister–Schreier theorem (`Sec8/A/RS.lean`, `thm410_of`). Hypothesis (a),
  `item_a`, is proved in `A/ItemAProof` by fresh frames and the contraction of two
  Hadamard letters. Hypothesis (b), `item_b`, is proved in `A/ItemBProof` through
  Clément's relations (`QCert.Clement2019`, syntactically equivalent to Figure 6):
  the congruence of Claim B contains (a1)–(d2) by frames (`A/ItemBCon`), Clément's
  (20) from Equation (45) (`A/ItemB20`), Figure 6's (d3) by the derivation of
