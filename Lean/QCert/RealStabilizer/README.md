# Makary, Ross and Selinger, *Generators and relations for real stabilizer operators* (QPL 2021)

Lean formalization of Table 1's real stabilizer row: the reduced relations
R1–R16 of Figure 1 (Definition 5.6) present the group generated by `-1`,
`H`, `Z`, `CZ` on every number of qubits, as real orthogonal matrices
(Propositions 4.15 and 5.7).

## Statement (what a reviewer checks)

| Paper | Lean |
|---|---|
| gates −1, H, Z, CZ (§2) | `Syntax.lean`: `Gate` |
| Figure 1, R1–R16 | `Syntax.lean`: `Rel.R1`–`Rel.R16`, on the wires each is drawn on |
| matrices of §2 | `Semantics.lean`: `matH`, `matZ`, `matCZ`, `S : GateSem Gate ℝ 2` |
| main theorem | `Theorem.lean`: `presentation (n) : IsSubPresentationOf (Theory n) (S.gen (n := n))`, into `unitaryGroup (State 2 n) ℝ`, the orthogonal group `O(2ⁿ)` |

**Wires.** Figure 1 is drawn at heights `y = 0, 1.6, 3.2`; the bottom wire
is our wire `0`.  `CZ` is symmetric.  Circuits are in operator order;
`tseq` writes a diagram in time order.  Gates at the same horizontal
position act on disjoint wires and are listed bottom wire first.

## What is proved

* **Soundness** of every relation (`Soundness.lean`), decided exactly by the
  state-vector simulator `QCert.Sim` (from `SelingerClifford/Sim.lean`) over
  ℤ[√2] (Mathlib's `ℤ√2`) with `√2 H` in place of `H`, and carried to ℝ.
* **Grouplike** (`Theorem.lean`).
* **Distinct normal forms denote distinct operators** (Propositions
  4.10–4.15, `Unique.normal_unique'`).  `Embed.lean` reads a real word as a
  Clifford word (`-1 = ω⁴`, `Z = S²`) and proves that its real matrix, read
  in ℂ, is the matrix of that word (`sem_embW`), so the Pauli action of
  `SelingerClifford` (`actW`, conjugation by the matrix, with its injectivity
  and locality lemmas) is used unchanged.  `NormalForm.lean` has the typed
  derived generators and Definitions 4.6–4.9 (a Z-circuit carries its typing:
  `C` takes a simple wire); `Unique.lean` decides the actions of the boxes
  on their own wires (`fA` … `fEZ`, Proposition 4.10), gives explicit
  encodings for Propositions 4.11–4.13 and does Proposition 4.15 by
  induction on the width, the scalar `±1` being read off the matrix.
* **The typed relations of Appendix B from R1–R16** (`Relations*.lean`,
  generated by `gen/generate.py`, see below): the supplement's lemmas
  (`Lemmas.lean`, with their upside-down versions), the supplement's
  derivations of the typed relations (`Typed*.lean`), and the 139 relations
  as stated (`rel1` … `rel139`, numbered in the order of the paper's
  figures; all but `rel71`, see below), each with a checked derivation (`Holds.of_derives`, as in
  `SelingerClifford`; `Letters.lean`, `Rewrite.lean`).  Since every letter is
  an involution, a relation also holds in every rotated, reversed or split
  form of its relator (`Relator.lean`: `Holds.variant`), which the
  supplement uses freely.

## The generators (`gen/`)

`python3 generate.py` (Python 3 with PyMuPDF) rewrites `Boxes.lean`,
`Axioms.lean`, `Lemmas.lean`, `Typed*.lean` and `Relations*.lean`;
`--check` compares with the files on disk, and `--cache FILE` keeps the
derivations between runs.  It reads the paper's TeX source (`realstab.tex`
with `relations.tex`, from the arXiv source of 2109.05655) and the
supplement (`supplement.pdf`), which are not committed.

* `tex.py` parses the paper's `qcircuit` figures (gates, boxes, wire types
  from `greengrid`/`redgrid`); `paper.py` reads Figure 1, the definitions of
  the derived generators and Appendix B, and checks circuits numerically
  (`same`).
* `suppdraw.py` reads the supplement's derivations off the PDF's vector
  graphics, line by line with their labels.
* `relations.py` lists the 139 typed relations as used, with the
  corrections below, and checks every one numerically.
* `engine.py`, `derive.py` find each derivation step: a rewrite by the
  cited equation up to commuting letters, in any form of its relator; where
  a drawn line is not the operator of its neighbours (a slip of the
  supplement), it is skipped; where the supplement's derivation does not go
  through, the relator `l r⁻¹` is collapsed to the empty word by a
  best-first search (`collapse`).

In the supplement, some intermediate lines are misdrawn (they do not denote
the operator of their neighbours: Lemma 13 line 5, Lemma 16 line 9, (5.7)
line 2, (6.4), (6.13)); its statements (9.3) and (9.15) are false while the
paper's are true, (4.36) lacks a sign, and some labels name no equation
(`R18`, `11.x`).  The generator skips such lines and derives the paper's
statements.
