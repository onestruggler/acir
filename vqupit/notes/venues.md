# PLDI 2027 submission requirements

Checked 12 November 2026 against the
[official research-paper call](https://pldi27.sigplan.org/track/pldi-2027-papers).
This replaces the earlier OOPSLA-first venue survey.

- Deadline: 12 November 2026, 23:59 AoE (13 November, 12:59 in Amsterdam).
- Main text: at most 20 pages, including AI Use.
- Format: ACM acmsmall; the call recommends screen, review, anonymous,
  and nonacm. Do not change the font or text block to meet the limit.
- Excluded from the limit: references, supplementary appendices, and the
  Data-Availability Statement.
- Include a section titled Data-Availability Statement immediately before
  the references, describing the artifact, its limits, and evaluation plans.
- Include identifiable AI Use disclosure in the main text when AI was
  used for code, proofs, data, tests, or other research artifacts.
- Provide anonymized mechanized proofs with the initial submission.
- Omit author identities from the submission and anonymize supplementary
  materials; cite public prior work in the third person.
- The reserve-reviewer policy requires an eligible senior author to
  register unless an exemption applies; see the call for its conditions.

The manuscript and README implement or record these requirements.
Preparing the anonymous proof archive and completing the submission
form remain separate author steps.
