\section*{Data-Availability Statement}

The artifact is an Agda development containing the theorem implementations,
their dependencies, the literate paper sources, and build instructions.
\texttt{MainTheorems.agda} is the entry point for checking the results;
it requires Agda~2.8.0 and standard library~2.4.  The artifact's scope is
the algebraic presentation theorems described in
\S\ref{sec:discussion}; it does not include a verified unitary-matrix
interpretation or compiler performance experiments.  The repository
link is omitted for double-blind review.  We intend to submit the
artifact for PLDI artifact evaluation if the paper is accepted.
