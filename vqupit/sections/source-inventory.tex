\section{Artifact Source Inventory}\label{sec:source-inventory}

Table~\ref{tab:stats} measures retained sources, including comments and
blank lines, excluding the external standard library and literate
excerpts. The counts are reproduced by
\texttt{python3} \path{vqupit/notes/stats.py}. Roughly 40\% of the source remains
in the symplectic layer and 21\% in projective composition and
translations. The latter cost is substantial even though there is no
second large normalizer. These counts describe the proof's distribution;
they do not measure saved effort, runtime, or generality.
