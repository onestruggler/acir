A recent paper by Bian, Li, Ross, van de Wetering, and Zhao gives a
complete set of sixteen rewrite rules for multi-qudit Clifford circuits
in every odd prime dimension $p$: two circuits over the gates $H$, $S$,
and $CZ$ implement the same unitary exactly when the rules prove them
equal.  Its completeness proof is a normal-form argument whose
supporting derivations run to roughly a hundred pages.  This paper
reports a machine-checked proof of the result, in safe, axiom-free
Agda, against an independently constructed semantics, together with the
method that made it feasible.  The formalisation follows the paper's
proof for the symplectic factor, where the paper does its real work,
and departs from it at the step that promotes symplectic completeness
to projective completeness, which the paper performs ``up to Paulis''.
We instead factor the projective Clifford group as a semidirect product
of the projective Pauli group by the symplectic group, present the two
factors separately --- the symplectic one by a unique normal form,
realised as a verified tower of coset tables in the style of
Reidemeister and Schreier --- and glue the presentations with a
generic, verified semidirect-product theorem.  Presentation-level
``plumbing'' then reduces the result to the paper's own generators and
rules, a central extension restores the scalars $\omega^t$, and a direct
factor $\langle -1\rangle$ the sign, so that the paper's Figure~1 itself,
over $-\omega$, $H$, $S$, $CZ$, is verified to present the Clifford group.
Everything is
parametric in $p$ and typechecks with no postulates; the identification
of the semantic groups with the groups of unitary matrices is the one
step taken from the literature.
