# PathSum/CLAUDE.md

Guidance for working in `PathSum/`, the formalisation of M. Amy, *Towards Large-scale Functional Verification of Universal Quantum Circuits* (QPL 2018). The repository's top-level `CLAUDE.md` covers the presentation library, the toolchain and the conventions; this file is the path-sum development's module map, its departures from the paper, and its pitfalls — several of which cost minutes and gigabytes when ignored.

## Overview and typechecking

Amy's path-sum calculus (QPL 2018). The core is §4 — lemmas 4.1–4.3, and corollary 4.4 for circuits over {H, S, CZ} as the paper *proves* it, about the circuit itself and syntactically (`corollary-4-4-any`, `corollary-4-4-syntactic`); its polynomial-time statement is proved in a cost model over sparse path-sums (`Cost/Corollary`). Around it: lemma 2.5 in general; composition (def. 2.6, prop. 2.7, remark 2.8 up to renaming path variables); the paper's gate set {H, CNOT, R_k} (def. 2.9, props. 2.10 and 2.14); ℤ[ζ] as a ring, definition 2.4, and unitarity of every circuit over both gate sets; all of figure 2 ([Case], Boolean-valued quotients) at any variables, with decidable matching; corollary 4.4 by the paper's Gaussian-elimination route and under any rules in any order; lemma 4.1/corollary 4.4 up to a global phase; equivalence of two circuits by the miter; the Clifford hierarchy of section 2 (the gates' levels, C₃ not closed, one-qubit C₂); and the worked examples (at M₀ = 0). **Not reached by `MainTheorems.agda`**; its own root is `PathSum/Theorems.agda`, which covers the whole directory. Typecheck it with `wsl --exec /usr/bin/agda +RTS -M6G -RTS PathSum/Theorems.agda` — **always under a heap cap** (an uncapped run once exhausted the WSL VM). From scratch the directory takes ~30 min CPU and peaks near 4.5 GB; the root alone ~160 s. (`Denotation` and `Cyclotomic` needed 9–10 GB and 1.5–4 min each until October 2026, when their `with`s over amplitude goals became helpers; each now checks in under 10 s.) The slowest modules are closed examples — `Examples/CaseIdentity` ~150 s, `HiddenShift/Example` ~145 s, `Examples/Restrict/NonLinear` ~70 s — and the root; then the `Hierarchy` modules at 40–70 s each, and everything else under 30 s (`--profile=modules` on the root lists them).

## Modules

- **`Polynomial.agda`** / **`Polynomial/Properties.agda`**: multilinear polynomials over the dyadic rationals. `Mon n m = Subset n × Subset m` (input and path variables), `Poly n m = Mon n m → ℤ` (numerators over 2^M), `Σsub`/`Σmon`, `eval`, `liftXor` (the lifting of a Z₂-linear form), `subst`. Properties holds lemma 2.5 (`liftXor-value`), the evaluation homomorphism, and the substitution theory: `eval-subst`, `eval-split`, `eval-subst-fixed`, `Σsub-at`/`Σmon-at` (a sum splits at *any* index, not only the head), `liftXor-split`, `hh-case`.
- **`Order.agda`**: the order of a phase polynomial (def. 2.11) as a 2-adic divisibility predicate, and lemma 2.13 (`subst-Ord≤`).
- **`Base.agda`**: `record PathSum (n k m : ℕ)`, `idPS`, `y₀`, `head-part`/`tail-part`, `Internal`.
- **`Reduction.agda`**: `⅛`/`¼`/`½`, the reducts, and `_⟶_` with constructors `elim`, `ω`, `hh` — each on the first path variable, with Z₂-linear quotients (`liftXor c S`), which is all lemma 4.3 needs. `[Case]` and general quotients are in `Reduction/General`, a second relation; this one is left alone because `Denotation` proves it sound.
- **`Mobius.agda`**: Möbius inversion modulo c — a multilinear polynomial whose value at every Boolean point is divisible by c has every coefficient divisible by c (`values⇒coefficients`, and `values⇒coefficientsᵐ` for any number of path variables).
- **`Cyclotomic.agda`**: ℤ[ζ] = ℤ[X]/(X^H+1) with `H = 2^(2+M₀)`, `N = 2H`, `c = N/8`. `Amp = Fin H → ℤ`, `_≐_`, `zpow`, `rot`, `√2·`, `scale`, `Σᴮ` (sums over assignments), `Σᴮ-at`, `scale-injective`, and the coordinate facts `zpow0-at-0`, `√2·zpow0-at-c`, `2·≢scale-zpow0`.
- **`Denotation.agda`** (checked `--call-by-name`): `amp`, `hits`, `_≋_`, the three soundness proofs, lemma 4.2, the undersized lemmas, and `semantics`. Layered in modules by *what premise they need*: `Branches ξ eqf` (the pair of y₀ branches, no premise), `Cancel ξ c S eqP eqf` (head ≈ ½·form), `ωBranches` (head ≈ ¼ + ½·form). Each re-exports the one below with `open … public`.
- **`Semantics.agda`**: the interface `record Semantics` — `_≋_` and its equivalence, `⟶-sound`, `interference`, `undersized-elim`, `undersized-ω`.
- **`Clifford.agda`**: §4.3 over that interface — `progress`, `lemma-4-3`, `corollary-4-4`, and `⟶*-sound`, proposition 3.1 along a whole chain.
- **`Circuit.agda`**: the two hypotheses of corollary 4.4, discharged. Clifford circuits over `H`, `S`, `CZ` (`Gate`, `Circuit`), and `⟦_⟧ᴿ`, the isometry restriction of §4.1 already reified — a Hadamard allocates a path variable only when a later one touches its wire, since otherwise `f (x , y) = x` forces that variable to be `x_w`. Hence `norm C` counts every Hadamard while `paths C` counts only those, and `⟦⟧ᴿ-Internal`/`⟦⟧ᴿ-Ord≤` are theorems. Also `⟦_⟧`, the unrestricted path-sum of definition 2.9 (`runᵁ`: every Hadamard allocates), with `pathsᵁ≡norm`, and the public `run-sig`/`⟦⟧ᴿ-sig` (every output of `⟦ C ⟧ᴿ` is its input). Semantics-free: it imports only `Base`, `Order`, `Polynomial` (with `Properties`) and `Reduction` (for `¼`/`½`).
- **`Assign.agda`**: shared by `AssignSum`, `CircuitAmp`, `CircuitSemantics`, `Isometry`, `Decide` and `Syntactic` — `_[_≔_]` (overwrite one bit) and its pointwise laws, `_=ᵇ_`, `same` (two assignments agree everywhere), `[_]ᶻ`.
- **`Norm.agda`**: the trace form on ℤ[ζ], `⟪ a , b ⟫ = Σ_{j<H} coeff a j · coeff b j` and `‖ a ‖² = ⟪ a , a ⟫` (= Tr(a·ā)/H). Positive definite (`‖‖²-zero`), preserved by every `rot` (`⟪⟫-rot`, via a periodic-window lemma on `coeff`), `parallelogram`, `‖‖²-√2` (the cross term vanishes because multiplication by i is antisymmetric), hence `‖‖²-scale` and `‖zpow0‖²`.
- **`AssignSum.agda`**: `Σᶻ`, integer sums over all assignments — **opaque** (a transparent one stuck on an unknown `k` leaves implicit `k`s unsolvable); use `Σᶻ-zero`/`Σᶻ-suc` and the lemmas `Σᶻ-point` (split off one assignment), `Σᶻ-at` (pair the two values of a bit), `Σᶻ-≥0`, `Σᶻ-≡0`.
- **`CircuitAmp.agda`**: `toPS`/`ampˢ`, the path-sum and amplitudes of an interpretation state, and one lemma per gate (`ampˢ-S`, `ampˢ-CZ`, `ampˢ-allocH`, `ampˢ-finalH`, `ampˢ-init`): each gate changes the amplitudes by its own unnormalised matrix.
- **`CircuitSemantics.agda`**: `Column`, `δ`, `gateᴬ`, `applyᴬ` (the circuit's matrix, gate by gate); `prop-2-10` (`amp ⟦ C ⟧ x z ≐ applyᴬ C (δ x) z`), `⟦⟧-unit-columns` (every column of `⟦ C ⟧` has trace-form norm 2^(norm C); orthogonality is `Unitarity`), `⟦⟧ᴿ-restricts` (same diagonal as `⟦ C ⟧`) and `⟦⟧ᴿ-diagonal`.
- **`Decide.agda`**: the test on a fully reduced path-sum, input by input. `id-if′` (no normalisation, the single path returns every x, phase ≡ 0 mod 2^M at every x ⇒ identity), its converse `id-no-norm`/`id-only-if′` from the refutations, together `id⇔′`; `search`, a finite search over assignments for any decidable property that respects pointwise equality; and `decide-≋-id : Dec (ξ ≋ idPS)` for `ξ` without path variables (decidability as such is elementary — finitely many entries).
- **`Syntactic.agda`**: the converse of `Identity.id-if` — a path-sum without path variables that is the identity has no normalisation, outputs the inputs mod 2 and phase 0 mod 2^M, *coefficient by coefficient* (`id-only-if`, via `Decide.id-no-norm`/`id-only-if′`, the mod-2 reading `outBit-∣`, and `Mobius`); together `id⇔syntactic`.
- **`Isometry.agda`**: lemma 4.1 for any path-sum. `WellFormed ξ` = every column has trace-form norm ≤ 2^k (implied by definition 2.4: `PartialIsometry.PartialIsometric⇒WellFormed`; strictly weaker); `Restriction-id ξ` = `amp ξ x x ≐ scale k (zpow 0ℤ)` for all x (the restricted sum is the normalised 1); `lemma-4-1`, and `diagonal-≋` for path-sums none of whose paths leaves its input.
- **`Identity.agda`**: what `Reduces` stops short of — whether a path-sum with no path variables left is the identity. Such a sum has a single path, so its amplitude is `0` or the single power `ζ^P(x)`, and the question is one about coordinates in ℤ[ζ]: `id-if` (the criterion — no normalisation, outputs the inputs mod 2, phase `0` mod `2^M`, stated coefficient-wise) and `not-id-out`, `not-id-norm`, `not-id-phase` (the three ways it fails, each at one input). Coefficient-wise vs pointwise means these four do not visibly cover every case; `Decide.id⇔′` is the pointwise characterisation, and `Syntactic.id⇔syntactic` (Möbius inversion) shows `id-if` is also necessary. The refutations rest on `zpow≢scale`, that a power of ζ is never `√2^(1+j)`, proved by rotating back to `ζ^0` and appealing to `Cyclotomic.2·≢scale-zpow0`.
- **`Corollary.agda`**: the circuit-level proofs the root restates (`circuit-≋-id`, `circuit-WellFormed`, `lemma-4-1-circuit`, `reduct≋`, `circuit-id`/`circuit-not-id`, `corollary-4-4-⟦⟧`/`-any`/`-syntactic`, `circuit-decidable`, `matrix-decidable`), kept out of the root so other modules can import them — **no module in the directory may import `Theorems`**.
- **`Polynomial/Product.agda`**, **`Polynomial/Substitution.agda`**, **`Polynomial/Boolean.agda`**: the product `_*ᴾ_` (`eval-*ᴾ`, `monoᴾ`); substitution of any polynomial for a variable (`substᴾ`, `eval-substᴾ`, `Absent`, the quarters `q₀₀`…`q₁₁` that [Case] reads); Boolean-valued polynomials (`BoolValued`, `_⊕ᴾ_`, `¬ᴾ`, `BExp`/`liftᵉ`); lemma 2.5 for every Boolean polynomial (`liftᴮ`, `lemma-2-5`, `lift-unique`, `BoolValued⇔idem`).
- **`Reduction/General.agda`**: all of figure 2 at the head as `_⟶ᴳ_` — `elimᴳ`, `ωᴳ`/`hhᴳ` with Boolean-valued quotients, `caseᴳ` (removes two variables; its premises are the three quarter coefficients) — with `⟶⇒⟶ᴳ` and `⟶ᴳ*-bounded` (no longer exact). **`Reduction/Sound.agda`**, **`Reduction/CaseSound.agda`**, **`Pairs.agda`**: `⟶ᴳ-sound`, `⟶ᴳ*-sound` (appendix A). **`Reduction/GeneralCorollary.agda`**: the circuit verdicts along `⟶ᴳ` chains (`corollary-4-4-anyᴳ`, …).
- **`Interference.agda`**: lemma 4.2 for general quotients — `interferenceᴳ` (Q odd at some input: `¬ (Q ≈[ + 2 ] 0ᴾ)`), `interference-bool` (Boolean-valued and non-zero), and `lemma-4-2-as-stated-fails` (Q = 2x₁: "non-zero, integer-valued" is not enough — but its witness is untied, √2·id under k = m; the real refutation is `lemma-4-2-as-stated-fails-tied`, `PathSum 1 3 3`).
- **`Reorder.agda`**, **`Anywhere.agda`**: rules at any internal variable. `front j` lists y_j first (`/ʸ`, `∖ʸ`, `eval-front`, `Ord≤-front`); `Anywhere R` lifts a head relation (`plain` / `at j`), `_∪ᴿ_` unions them; `_⟶ᵍ_ = Anywhere _⟶_` with `elimAt`/`ωAt`/`hhAt` stating premises at y_j; exact length `⟶ᵍ*-length`, `SN`, `⟶ᵍ-SN`. ## What is proved, and what is not

No theorem rests on an assumption: the hypotheses of the corollary are proved by `Circuit`, the step back from the restriction by lemma 4.1 (`Isometry` + `CircuitSemantics`), and the final test by `Decide` + `Syntactic`. The polynomial time bounds (proposition 3.2, corollaries 2.15 and 4.4, the abstract's equivalence procedure) are proved in a cost model, not a machine model, by `Cost/*` — on sparse path-sums, for fixed order and the linear rules, corollary 4.4's for {H, S, CZ} and, by Gaussian elimination, for {H, CNOT, R_k} at level ≤ 2 — the dense development being exponential (polynomials are functions on all 2^(n+m) monomials). What is *not* formalised: running times on a machine and complexity classes; footnote 2's complexity conclusion (complexity classes, machine models, Cook–Levin and P = co-NP; its logical half is `Expand.unique⇒no-expansion`, and the reduction, certificate check and conditional are `Hardness/*`); and the benchmarks of section 5 as runs of the tool (the QFT, n-bit Toffoli, adder and hidden shift families are proved for every size, semantically).

## Departures from the paper

All are in the module headers; what is not formalised is in the section above. A path-sum carries its normalisation `k` **and** its path-variable count `m` separately, because def. 2.1 ties them but fig. 2's rules do not (so a counterexample to one of the paper's claims about path-sums should use `k = m`); the precision is fixed — phases are numerators over 2^M, M = M₀ + 3, and `R_k` for k > M reads as `R_M`; lemma 4.3's implicit side condition (the rule may cost more normalisation than the path-sum has) is *proved* here rather than assumed, via the coordinate facts above; the core corollary-4.4 development is over {H, S, CZ}, whose restriction is reified by construction (the paper's {H, CNOT, R_k} is `CRK/`, reaching the corollary by compilation and, as the paper does, by Gaussian elimination in `Gauss`); lemma 4.1 is proved under `WellFormed` (trace-form column norms), which definition 2.4 implies and which is strictly weaker; composition's laws and remark 2.8 hold up to ≋ and, with the wires re-indexed, as equalities up to renaming the path variables (`Compose/Monoidal`, `CRK/Structural`), not as equations (indices agree only propositionally, no funext; the renaming is genuinely needed); and four statements are false as printed and are proved corrected (or refuted, the last) beside counterexamples — lemma 4.2 needs Q odd at some input (`lemma-4-2-as-stated-fails-tied`), proposition 2.14's bound is max(2, k) (`prop-2-14-false-at-1`), and proposition 2.7's well-formedness claim fails for `WellFormed` and for definition 2.4 (`WellFormed-∘-fails`, `PartialIsometric-∘-fails-tied`; it holds after an isometry); and proposition 3.2's time bound fails for all of figure 2 beyond order 2, for path-sums written as sums of monomials (`Ladder/Size`: every normal form of the 4n-term ladder has an output of 2^n monomials). **Pitfall (closed circuits).** A statement `⟦ C ⟧ ≋ ζ` about a *closed* circuit must use a single instance of `PathSum.Circuit 3` / `PathSum.CRK.Circuit 3` (take them from `Examples.Base`), and must not apply a general lemma (`≋-trans`, `≋-by-eval`, `renumbered-≋`) to the term `⟦ C ⟧` directly when the other side has a numeral normalisation: Agda unfolds `≋` into amplitudes to compare the types — 40–107 s per fact for (SH)³ and the seven-T Toffoli, and over 10 minutes (abandoned) for the 27-gate controlled-T. State such facts through a name whose arguments are the circuit (`circuit-id!`, `crk-renumbered-≋`, `Implements C ζ`, …); each then takes about a second. The same holds for *restating* a theorem about a concrete (even wire-symbolic) circuit: spell it with the names the proving module uses. `Theorems`' `tof-spec` written with `K.⟦_⟧` (a `CRK.Circuit` application) and `Toffoli.Gate`'s `tof` ran > 3 min; with `CRK.Path`'s `⟦_⟧` and `Toffoli`'s re-exported `tof`, as `Toffoli` states it, 18 s. Statements stuck on a symbolic n (`Toffoliₙ n p`, `QFTC n`) are cheap either way. The copies a module application makes count as different names: a restated type that names closed terms through `module CE = X M₀`'s copies (`CE.P₀`) is compared, by unfolding, with the proof's type, which names the originals (`X.P₀ M₀`) — `PartialIsometric-∘-fails` cost 62 s of the root's 195 s that way and nothing once spelled `CEᵒ.P₀ M₀` (`import X as CEᵒ`). The same holds of the operators a module's types use through its own imports: those are its private copies, so `Hierarchy` names its applications (`module Opʰ = Operator M₀`, `Paʰ`) and the root spells `𝒞₃⇔generators` with `Hᵒ.Opʰ._·_ M₀` etc. (62 s → nothing). Profile with `--profile=definitions` on a renamed copy of the module (its imports load from interfaces) to find such cases.

**Pitfall (`with` over big goals).** Do not `with`-abstract when the goal mentions `≋` (or anything that unfolds to `amp`): Agda normalises the goal looking for the abstracted term. In `Decide` that cost 52 s and 8 GB; helpers taking a Boolean and its equation (`f : ∀ b → e ≡ b → Goal`, called `f e refl`) took 5 s and 0.6 GB. `Denotation` had three such `with`s (on `liftXor-value`, the quotient's 0/1 value): 165 s and 8.9 GB of live heap; as helpers taking the disjunction, 9 s and 0.4 GB. `Cyclotomic`'s `coeff` lemmas did `with classify w`: 94 s and 9.3 GB; as helpers over `Class w` applied to `classify w` (`coeff a w` is `coeff-aux a (classify w)` by definition), 6 s and 0.9 GB. Likewise `hits`/`eval-cong`'s implicit assignments are not inferable through `hits` (a defined function) — pass them.

**Pitfall.** `_≋_` matches on both normalisations, so nothing can be recovered through it by unification: every statement mentioning it must be given its path-sums explicitly (`≋-trans {ξ = a} {ζ = b} {χ = d}`), including in the `Semantics` record fields.

**Pitfalls (the root).** Import closed example modules with `as`, never as a module application (the copies make ≋ statements unfold); a qualified `X.f` of a parameterised module needs a plain `import X` even when `open import X M₀ using (…)` is there; take generic names (`at`, `prim`, `Layout`, `count`, …) qualified; the {H, CNOT, R_k} adjoint is `KA.†` (both `_†` are in scope) and ℤ's `_*_` is `_*ℤ_` (ℕ's is in scope); restate a theorem with the names its own module states it with (see *closed circuits* above).
